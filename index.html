Privacy Notice

What we collect

When you trigger an SOS or start a Safe Walk, Vigil shares your approximate location, the time, and your student identifier with Campus Protection Services and the trusted guardians you've added. Incident reports collect only what you choose to enter, and can be submitted anonymously.

Who can see it

Location and alert data during an active SOS or Safe Walk session is visible to Campus Protection Services and your chosen guardians only. It is not visible to other students or staff outside that response.

Data retention

Incident and alert records are kept only as long as needed to review and resolve the case, in line with NMU's data protection policy. This is a hackathon prototype - no data shown here is collected from a real device or real people.
